## Global Constraints

- Players are 8 and almost 5; the younger cannot read. Pictures only, touch-first, iPad Safari landscape, Minecraft look. Do not restyle what is built.
- Kid rules: every tap target ≥ 64 px on a 1024×768 iPad; a picture with every word (no text-only cue); every refusal buzzes.
- The sim rules do not change. Golden hash 3452586360, `tests/sim/determinism.test.ts` and `tests/sim/noTwistReplay.test.ts` stay green and untouched; every existing Adventure outcome of worlds 1–3 (levels, stand-in results, `KNOWN_MISSES` for p1..p3/z1..z3) stays as it is unless a task says so. New `BossKey`s extend tables only.
- No unit, hero, spell, Quick Battle, world-1..3 number changes. Tunable here: the 16 new levels and `Balance.bosses.yeti|glowcap` only.
- Zombie levels are Toby's: 2–3 minutes, forgiving; the beginner on Easy wins ≥ 50% on z4 (content design §4, `beginnerWinMin`).
- Each boss model ≤ 6,000 triangles. 16.6 ms p95 at DPR 2 on the M3 for both fights (Yeti in snow with its ice lanes, Glowcap in fog with fireflies), and no single frame over 50 ms after the entrance (the Pharaoh's first strike took 260 ms before the warm-up fix).
- Anything first drawn mid-fight (a geometry, a material, a shot, a warning look) is built at fight start and drawn once at no size (`BossFx.warmUp`, `BattleView.warmShots`); a boss's shots and warnings must not read as a twist tile (ice pans, mist) or as another card's effect.
- A lesson can never freeze: a twist pan ends on any tap, the "tap" glove shows after `TWIST_HINT_SECONDS` (3); an `after: 'foe'` step holds nothing until it arms; glove tiles are validated with `match.check`.
- Bands are not loosened; a miss is recorded in `KNOWN_MISSES` (`it.fails`) with its numbers per block. Tuning is confirmed on all three blocks (1, 1001, 2001) before a tuning commit, not on block 1 alone.
- No network requests to Firebase from tests or tools; every e2e/tool URL carries `cloud=fake`. No deploys by agents. No database rules change is expected; if one is, rules come from `tools/build-rules.mjs` only and `node tools/build-rules.mjs --check` exits 0.

## Rulings made while planning

1. **The plants' late curve (Needs Michal 1), decided: the default.** From p4-4 on, each plant level is tuned to the lowest average-player win the expert floor (≥95%) allows; the win-band miss is recorded in `KNOWN_MISSES`, and his 3-star share is held to the zombie-style star band instead (`plantStarBand`, ruling 2). p4-1..p4-3 stay in the win band (reachable through about i 27).
2. **`plantStarBand(def: Pick<LevelDef, 'side' | 'world' | 'n' | 'kind'>): WinBand | null`**: null for zombies and for i < 28; else `averageStarBand(i)`, with `lo` 5 lower on a boss (as the plants' boss win band). Applies to p4-4..p4-8 now and p5 later. The win band tests stay as they are.
3. **Block-aware misses.** `KNOWN_MISSES` keys may end ` @<first>` (`' @1001'`): the miss applies to that block only; a plain key applies to all blocks, as today. Block-dependent misses (p3-7's, three of step 3's) are then recorded exactly. Step-3 entries are not re-keyed.
4. **`child` is a recorded signal, not a gate.** The band tests gate on a fixed `GATED_STAND_INS = ['beginner', 'average', 'expert']`; `record` prints every stand-in in `LEVEL_STAND_INS`. If the `human-standin` branch has merged by Task 8 (`LEVEL_STAND_INS` includes `'child'`), z4's child columns are printed and reported; if not, Task 8 says so and the e2e walk (Task 9) is the only child signal. A child result never enters an `expect` or a `KNOWN_MISSES` key.
5. **Boss hp ranges are guides** (Yeti 6500–9000, Glowcap 10000–14000). Where the expert floor or the 5-minute cap fails inside the range, go outside it and annotate content design §2 with the shipped hp (step 3: Cactus King 6200).
6. **Breakthrough clocks** (z4-3, z4-6) start at 240 s and may go to 300 s if three stars at 240 s are out of the star band; each deviation is written in the level's comment, pinned in `z4.test.ts` and listed in the README and the Task 7 report. A longer clock is kept over a shorter Toby level (step 3: 275 / 255 s).
7. **The Yeti's summons land in random lanes** (the sim's zombie summon picks lanes at random; no sim change). "Snow pups in lanes 0 and 4" is carried by p4-8's scripted Very Fast Walker trickle (as p3-8).
8. **Pattern numbers the spec leaves open**, as the world-2/3 cadence: Yeti slam 380 / 160, reach 0.6, wind-up 1.5 s; phase-2 and phase-3 strike every 6 s, summons every 5 s; the Yeti's avalanche leaves `stun` 2 s, damage 80, `count` 1 then 2; Glowcap's spore cloud every 6 s, barrage 4 every 7 s, `splash` 1.0 on phase 1's and 4's spore bombs. Tunable in Tasks 6–7 with hp.
9. **Yeti shield:** `{ atHealth: [0.66, 0.33], seconds: 6, damageFactor: 0.5, bouncesStraight: true }` (the dome recoloured, so peas ping off it and lobs get through at ×0.5 — the Mango-pult answer). If the expert cannot hold ≥95% inside the hp guide, flip `bouncesStraight` to false before raising hp.
10. **New `BossShows` variants:** `shield: 'snow'` (a dome tinted snow white and ice blue, handled with `dome`/`moon`/`sand` in `BossFx.sync`) and `shield: 'cap'` (plates `capL`/`capR`, handled with `bark`/`ice`); `barrage: 'sporeBomb'` (glowing spore bomb, own pool in `BattleView`, `throwsSporeBombs`). The Yeti has no `summonLook` (its phase-3 Skeleton Warriors would take wolf ears) and no new blow look: its avalanche uses `strikeWarnings.stun` colours and a white `blow`. The Yeti's `phaseParts: ['frostBreath']` shows from its first mark; phase 3 has no look of its own until step 5's per-phase parts.
11. **The fog pan on the zombie side** (`TwistTiles`, `kind: 'fog'`, `side: 'zombies'`): the Peashooter ghost and its pea are as on the plant side; a ghost Brain Eater walks in from the right to the puff point, the pea puffs in front of it, and it walks on to the Peashooter untouched; one loop. The level is built as specified; a test measures which columns reach the biters; wall column 6 → 7 and shooters 0–1 → 0 are permitted tuning levers (recorded).
14. **Free Play** with ten cards a side is re-measured and recorded in Task 8, not tuned (content design §7 risk 6).
15. **Order.** Tasks run sequentially in one worktree in the order 1, 4, 5, 2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9 (step 3's ledger order; no parallel implementers). e2e file names are `e2e/tundra-plants.spec.ts` and `e2e/meadow-zombies.spec.ts`, both copied in shape from `e2e/desert.spec.ts` (`tundra.spec.ts` and `meadow.spec.ts` are the other sides' world 2).
